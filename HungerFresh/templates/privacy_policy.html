Privacy Policy

Your trust is the secret ingredient in every meal we deliver. 🧡

At HungerFresh, we value your privacy like we value fresh ingredients. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your data—so you can order with complete peace of mind.

To serve you better and create personalized experiences, we may collect the following information:

  • Device & Usage Data
Section 2

How We Use Your Information

Your data helps us create a delicious and seamless experience:

  • Process and deliver your orders accurately
  • Improve our menu recommendations & service quality

Data Protection

We protect your information like we protect our secret recipes: 🔐 Industry-standard encryption for all transactions, strict access controls for sensitive data, regular security audits, and secure storage protocols. Your privacy is non-negotiable.

Section 4

Trusted Partners

We work with carefully selected partners for payment processing, analytics, and delivery. Every partner signs strict confidentiality agreements and follows the same high security standards we uphold. 🛡️

Section 5

Your Rights

You're in complete control of your data. You have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal information
  • Update or correct your details anytime
  • Request deletion of your account data
  • Opt-out of marketing communications
  • Export your order history

Just contact us and we'll guide you through the process—no hassle, no hidden steps.

Section 6

Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies to remember your preferences, keep you logged in, and improve our service. You can manage cookie settings in your browser anytime. We never sell your browsing data to third parties. 🚫

We may update this policy occasionally to reflect service improvements or legal changes. Updates take effect upon posting. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of HungerFresh means you accept the latest version.
